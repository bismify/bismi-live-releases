# Bismi Live

**Turn your phone into a live gaming channel.** Bismi Live streams your mobile gameplay straight to YouTube — no PC, no capture card, no complicated setup. Pick your game, tap start, and you are live.

## Why you will love it

- **One-tap live streaming** of your Android gameplay to YouTube, right from your phone
- **Floating on-screen widget** keeps your stream controls one tap away while you play, so you never break your flow
- **Crystal-clear game audio**, with an optional microphone overlay for live commentary
- **Built for real life:** network drops and connection changes are handled with automatic reconnection, so a Wi-Fi blip does not end your broadcast
- **Smart session handling:** abandoned streams are ended automatically, so you never leave a dead stream running on your channel
- **Always up to date:** a built-in in-app upgrade flow keeps every installation on the latest version, no Play Store needed
- **A modern, dark, distraction-free interface** designed for long streaming sessions

## Download

Get the latest version from the releases page:

**https://github.com/bismify/bismi-live-releases/releases/latest**

Download the newest APK asset, open it on your phone, and follow the install prompt (Android may ask you to allow installs from that source the first time — this is normal for apps installed outside a store).

## Requirements

- Android 10 or newer
- A YouTube channel with live streaming enabled (enable it in YouTube Studio if you have not yet — activation can take up to 24 hours the first time)

## Privacy

Your stream goes directly from your phone to YouTube. Bismi Live has no ads and no trackers, and your settings stay on your device.
